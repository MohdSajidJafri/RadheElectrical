## 3. Target Audience & Personas
1. **Residential Homeowner (Dausa & nearby towns):**
   - *Motivations:* High monthly power bills in summer, frequent voltage fluctuations, desire for energy self-sufficiency, long-term rooftop asset.
   - *Pain Points:* Fear of cheap low-efficiency panels, uncertain installation quality, absence of responsive local post-installation support.
   - *Core Need:* Clear guidance on 1KW to 10KW capacities, honest local installation, fast consultation.

2. **Commercial & Small Business Owner (Shops, Workshops, Hospitals, Warehouses, Schools):**
   - *Motivations:* Heavy daytime operational power load, cutting commercial tariff overheads, reliable 3-phase solar inverter systems.
   - *Pain Points:* Installation downtime, structural rooftop safety, complex equipment maintenance.
   - *Core Need:* High-capacity system sizing (5KW - 10KW+), high-yield inverters, professional workmanship.

## 4. Key Value Propositions & Trust Pillars (No Fake Claims)
- **Professional Installation:** Precision structure mounting, optimized azimuth/tilt angle, concealed conduit wiring, surge protection (SPD), and grounding earthing pits.
- **Quality Products:** High-efficiency Mono PERC / TOPCon panels, Tier-1 grid-tie / hybrid inverters, corrosion-resistant GI structures.
- **Appropriate Price:** Right-sized system design matching exact load profile without unnecessary over-capacity upsell.
- **After-Sales Support:** Proactive maintenance, inverter diagnostics, string monitoring, and rapid local turnaround in Dausa.
- **Free Consultation:** Zero-obligation site survey, rooftop shade assessment, and tailored system quote.
