# RADHE ELECTRICAL — Art Direction & Visual System V2

## 1. Creative POV: "Industrial Solar Architecture & Rajasthan Rooftops"
A fusion of high-end architectural photography, industrial engineering discipline, and authentic local Rajasthan ground truth.

### Key Visual Tenets:
1. **Section Pacing & Tonal Rhythm:** Alternating between warm sun-baked mineral stone canvases (`#FBFBF9`, `#F3F1EA`) and deep obsidian/graphite night fields (`#0A0D14`, `#12151E`) to create dramatic visual contrast rather than a monotonous dark SaaS canvas.
2. **Typography as Architecture:** Bold, oversized display typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) paired with crisp, spacious editorial sans (`Inter`).
3. **Photography as the Structural Anchor:** Expansive architectural photographs of actual galvanized steel structures, solar arrays under the Rajasthan sun, and authentic rooftop transformations.
4. **Restrained Saffron Accent:** Warm solar saffron (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`) used strictly as a precise point of conversion, never plastered indiscriminately over every label or background.
5. **Zero Template Slop:** No generic cards, no floating pills, no uppercase kicker badges, no artificial "dashboard" widgets.

## 3. Typographic Scale & Composition
- **Display Hero:** `clamp(3rem, 7vw, 5.75rem)` / Weight: `800` / Tracking: `-0.035em` / Leading: `1.02`
- **Display Section:** `clamp(2.25rem, 5vw, 3.75rem)` / Weight: `800` / Tracking: `-0.025em` / Leading: `1.1`
- **Sub-headline / Statement:** `clamp(1.125rem, 2vw, 1.35rem)` / Weight: `400` / Leading: `1.6`
- **Numeral Hero:** `clamp(2.5rem, 6vw, 4.5rem)` / Weight: `800` / Tracking: `-0.03em`
- **Body:** `1rem` (16px) & `1.125rem` (18px) / Leading: `1.65`
- **Micro Detail:** `0.75rem` (12px) / Tracking: `0.08em` / Uppercase
